    Machine Learning Lab
    Train models on your uploaded dataset. Results are evaluated on held-out rows and should be reviewed before business decisions.

    1. Sales / Numeric Prediction

    Random Forest Regression

    Select a numeric target such as Sales or Revenue. The model learns from other columns and reports hold-out accuracy; this is not a guaranteed future forecast.

    2. Anomaly Detection

    Isolation Forest

    Find unusual records from selected numeric columns. An anomaly is a review flag, not proof of fraud or an error.

    3. Customer / Record Segmentation

    K-Means Clustering

    Group records by similarity across at least two numeric features. If customer-level data is not present, groups represent rows rather than identified customers.

    Machine-learning features require the Flask backend and scikit-learn.
